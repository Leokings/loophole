**0:15–0:35 — The law.** Open the law panel and show the narrow rule: “No faction may UNDERMINE a faction that holds public office.” Show the certification status and stance tally, then explain that the contract counts free stances and activates the law after its effective round.

**0:35–0:55 — Safe harbor.** Open the first completed case for a non-officeholder target. Show the `NOT_COVERED` label, the exact quote highlighted in the cited law, and the holding key. Explain that this is a loophole created by the rule's narrow wording.

**0:55–1:10 — Reuse.** Open the later matching case. Show `SUMMARY`, the link to the original holding, and its case ID. Explain that the same key is decided from stored precedent without a second model judgment.

**1:10–1:30 — Enforcement.** Show the officeholder breach and its `COVERED_BREACH` holding. Point to the contract-derived `VIOLATION` / `REPRIMAND` outcome and the finality state. Do not imply an effect is complete while the interface says it is pending.

**1:30–1:45 — Close the loophole.** Show amendment v2 and a post-amendment first impression. The new law ID creates a new key, so the earlier safe harbor remains historical while the new broader rule can cover the action.

**1:45–2:00 — Evidence.** Open the recorded proof panel or Case Law transaction links if `deploy/proof.json` exists. Distinguish finalized historical transactions from current live reads. If no proof manifest is available, say that the page is a local demonstration and do not present sample records as live evidence.
